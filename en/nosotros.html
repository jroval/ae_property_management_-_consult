The story

An unwavering commitment to dedication.

Our origin reflects an unwavering commitment to dedication.

It began with the trust built alongside clients who needed a dependable partner on the island to manage their newly finished residence. While handling the final details of the build and carrying out preventive maintenance on the property, a service was born that more and more clients began to request.

Growing organically, always guided by our clients' needs, we have developed a complete management model tailored to each property and each client.

Today, we cover a broad range of needs so that owners never have to worry about a thing: from upkeep, maintenance of installations, gardens and cleaning, to exclusive personalised concierge services and the technical management of building and renovation work. We care for your home with the dedication, discretion and rigour it deserves.

— Andrea, founder of AE Property Management & Consult
